Magnetometer Calibration

A correct calibration reports the same field magnitude in every orientation, so fitting one needs samples from the whole sphere of directions. The shell below is that sphere, drawn around the device itself and seen from a camera fixed in the room — so the shell turns as you turn the device, while the ghost outline and the dashed leader line stay put. Those two are what you aim with; the shell is the score. Dashed hollow rings are the angles you are still missing; a solid disc's size is how many samples it holds. Press Start, then turn the device until the solid block lands inside the ghost.
