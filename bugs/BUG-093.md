## BUG-093 — Sidepane hierarchy gives grandchildren the same weight as children

**Date filed:** 2026-08-07  
**Area:** host/web UI  
**Status:** open

Grandchildren in a sidepane hierarchy have the same visual weight as direct children;
for example, `CAMERA & POSE` and `VIEW MODE` do not read as distinct levels. Direct
children should be stronger through typography, indentation, spacing, or grouping.
Review the semantic nesting and shared styles and add a two-level hierarchy regression.
