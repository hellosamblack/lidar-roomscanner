## BUG-092 — Selecting a capture does not surface the load/build pane

**Date filed:** 2026-08-07  
**Area:** host/web UI  
**Status:** open  
**Priority:** critical

Selecting an item in the Captures pane does not surface the pane containing its Load,
Build, and related actions. Selecting a capture must make that action pane visible and
selected without an unrelated navigation step. Add a browser regression asserting
that the pane and expected controls appear after selection.
