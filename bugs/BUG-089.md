## BUG-089 — Captures pane does not grow with the primary sidebar

**Date filed:** 2026-08-07  
**Area:** host/web UI  
**Status:** open

The Captures pane keeps a fixed or independently constrained width while the primary
sidebar grows, leaving unused space and causing avoidable wrapping. It should track
the sidebar's available width subject to a sensible minimum. Trace the layout
constraints, including resize and narrow-window behavior.
