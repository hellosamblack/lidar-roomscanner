# BUG-097 — Camera default position not at human eye level in splat/detailed modes

**Date filed:** 2026-08-08  
**Status:** open  
**Area:** host/web UI (camera framing, view initialization)  

## Summary

In splat reconstruction and SLAM/detailed modes, the initial camera position does not default to a human eye-level height (approximately 6 feet off the ground) positioned in the middle of the largest room. The camera framing makes it difficult to observe the reconstruction at a natural viewing distance and height.

## Observation

- When loading a splat reconstruction or detailed SLAM map, the camera initializes at an unspecified position
- The camera height does not correspond to standing human eye level (~1.8 m / 6 feet)
- The camera is not centered in the middle of the largest reconstructed space
- This contrasts with the intuitive viewing position a user would expect for a room-scale reconstruction

## Expected behavior

1. On entering Splat display mode with a loaded splat, the camera should position itself at approximately **6 feet (1.83 m) off the ground**
2. The camera's horizontal position should be at the **centroid of the largest room** in the reconstruction (estimated by the point-cloud or mesh extent)
3. The camera distance/offset should be tuned to show the full room in frame without excessive zoom or underutilization of screen space

## Root cause

Unknown. Suspected areas:
- The initial camera offset (`distance`, `height`, `rotation` in the framing config) is not being computed per-reconstruction for the splat/detailed modes
- The splat material's bounding box calculation may be unavailable or not used for framing
- The room centroid / mesh extent is not being extracted or passed to the camera framing logic

## Related

## Resolution

TBD — requires computing the splat/detailed mesh extent at load time, deriving the centroid and bounding radius, and positioning the camera at human eye level within that extent.
