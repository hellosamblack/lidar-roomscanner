## BUG-069 — the stationarity gate cannot fire on a tripod, and could not help if it did

**Status:** fixed 2026-08-06 (new accel ZUPT reaches the map; the old coherence gate stays
display-only) · **Area:** host/slam (`slam/motion.py`, `slam/mapper.py` `step`) · **Found by:**
BUG-067's tripod capture, 2026-08-02.

`StationarityGate` exists to stop the ICP translation estimate random-walking while the sensor sits
still — the owner's original "device is stationary → model should be too". It has two properties
that together make it useless for the case it is named after.

**1. It is structurally unable to fire during a pan.** The gate requires mean per-frame rotation
≤ `rot_ceiling_deg = 0.3`, on the documented reasoning that "during a real scan the user is almost
always rotating the sensor to aim at the scene, so any appreciable rotation means *actively
scanning, not still*". A tripod pan is the counterexample that assumption excludes by construction:
rotation with **zero** translation. The capture's three pans run at 25–31 °/s ≈ 0.8–1.0 °/frame,
3× over the ceiling, so the gate is off for exactly the 43% of the capture that produces
essentially all of the drift.

**2. It is display-only, so it could not protect the map anyway.** `Mapper.step` applies a True
verdict to `report_pose` alone; `self._t_prev` and the TSDF integration always use the raw ICP
pose. That is deliberate and documented ("a false hold can never corrupt the reconstruction"), and
it is the right call for a gate that can misfire — but it means the *reconstruction* has no
zero-velocity constraint at all, only the preview does.

Fixing this is not a threshold change. It needs a discriminator that separates "rotating in place"
from "rotating while walking" — the coherence test was meant to be it, and `rot_ceiling_deg` was
added precisely because coherence alone misfired on a scan's curved path. Doing it properly means
earning the right to let a hold reach the map, which is a bigger change than relaxing a constant.

---

**Fix (2026-08-06) — a second, accelerometer-based ZUPT tier that reaches the map.** The
discriminator the coherence gate could not be is the **accelerometer**: specific force is ~1 g at
rest regardless of orientation, so a pure pan reads 1 g (centripetal a = ω²r ≈ 0.005 g at a cm lever
arm) while walking adds gait force that pushes |a| off 1 g. On the tripod capture |a| = 9.795 m/s²
(1 g = 9.807, 0.12 % off — no scale/bias issue) with 96.9 % of frames inside a 4 % band. `motion.
ZuptDetector` trips after `zupt_window` consecutive in-band frames and, unlike `StationarityGate`,
is **NOT rotation-gated**, so it fires through a pan.

Accel alone is not safe, and the measurement proved it: steady walking is also ~1 g between
footfalls, so an accel-only gate froze the pose mid-stride on the coffee circuit — **76 lost frames,
a 59-frame freeze, 563 ICP escalations**. The fix is a **translation-coherence veto**: hold only when
accel says still AND the recent ICP increments are incoherent (a walk's increments are coherent, net
≈ path; a still sensor's jitter cancels). That is the accel signal supplying the pan-capable "not
translating" evidence and coherence vetoing genuine directed travel.

When it trips, `Mapper.step` freezes the **true** pose's translation at `t_prev` BEFORE it feeds
`_t_prev` and TSDF integration (`mapper.py`, between the ICP solve and integrate) — so the
reconstruction gets the zero-velocity constraint, not just the preview. The old coherence
`StationarityGate` is unchanged and remains display-only; the two are deliberately separate tiers
(a coherence hold can misfire, so it must never reach the map; the accel hold is physically grounded
and LiDAR-independent, so it may).

Matched ensembles (n=10, veto at 0.5), horizontal closure:

| capture | baseline | ZUPT+veto | tracking |
|---|---|---|---|
| tripod (truth ≈ 0) | 0.121 ± 0.069 | **0.102 ± 0.015** | 0 lost |
| coffee circuit | 0.735 ± 0.269 | **0.671 ± 0.198** | **0 lost** (accel-only: 76) |
| roomSweep | 2.593 ± 0.316 | 2.544 ± 0.338 | 1 lost |

Clean and beneficial on all three; the tripod's run-to-run spread collapses 4.6× **at the capture's
native heading**. (This is NOT heading-invariance: a null `graft_yaw` sweep with ZUPT on still swings
the tripod closure 0.104 at 0° → 2.44 at 90° → 2.55 at 180°, so BUG-070 remains open — ZUPT tightens
each heading's variance without removing the across-heading dependence.) **Shipped ON by default**
(owner decision) — `[slam] zupt_enabled` (+ `zupt_window`, `zupt_accel_tol_g`,
`zupt_coherence`); a no-op wherever stream 11 is absent, so it degrades gracefully. Needs the raw
IMU in `step`, plumbed through the CLI, the ensemble, Detailed (`showcase`), and Live
(`web.make_slam_feed` → `SlamRunner`/`SlamWorker`, via `SensorState.latest_imu_raw()`); the remote
backend accepts-and-ignores it (local/offline only).

**Honest limit.** The veto is deliberately conservative: a sustained pan's fabricated drift is partly
coherent, so it declines to hold through it, which is why the tripod's *path length* only falls
13.1 → 11.5 m (see BUG-067). The accel-only mode (`zupt_coherence=0`) crushes that to 3.5 m but is
unsafe on real motion; it is kept for A/B, not shipped. `zupt_coherence` is a tunable if a future
capture warrants trading some real-motion safety for more pan suppression.
