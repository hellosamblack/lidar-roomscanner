## BUG-088 — Capture and IR-monitor selectors wrap inside a word

**Date filed:** 2026-08-07  
**Area:** host/web UI  
**Status:** open

Selector labels in the Captures pane and IR monitors wrap across a single word or
break awkwardly because spacing is too tight. Labels should remain readable, with
adequate padding and word-boundary reflow at narrow widths. Audit the shared selector
styling and verify narrow and wide supported viewports.
